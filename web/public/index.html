How it works

Four steps from install to watching.

  1. 1
    Install FOX PLAYER on your phone or TV box.
  2. 2
    Open Setup in the app — note your Device ID and scan the QR code.
  3. 3
    On the upload page, enter your ID and pairing token, then submit your playlist.
  4. 4
    Back in the app, tap Reload playlist and start watching.
